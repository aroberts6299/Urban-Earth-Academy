What campers will explore

Six themes.
A world of connections.

Camp brings the Urban Earth learning themes into a summer format, with guided investigations, creative projects, outdoor experiences, and teamwork. Here’s a preview of the activities we’re planning.

01✳

Urban nature
& ecology

Notice the life around us. Explore plants, habitats, biodiversity, and how growing spaces fit into the city.

A possible discoveryCreate a habitat journal or investigate a growing space.
02≈

Water &
watersheds

Follow water through a neighborhood. Connect rainfall, runoff, local waterways, and environmental health.

A possible discoveryBuild a watershed model and map the path of rain.
03◎

Outdoor exploration
& skills

Build confidence learning outdoors through observation, navigation, field skills, and responsible outdoor habits.

A possible discoveryFollow a guided route and record the landmarks along the way.
04↺

Environmental
stewardship

Investigate how native plants, pollinators, waste reduction, and everyday choices can support shared spaces.

A possible discoveryDesign a small stewardship action with a team.
05☀

Renewable energy
& our environment

Explore solar energy and connect simple STEM experiments to real applications and green career possibilities.

A possible discoveryTest a solar model under different light conditions.
06↗

Youth environmental
action

Bring discoveries together in a team project or presentation that shares a local idea and the learning behind it.

A possible discoveryCreate a project that helps others see their environment differently.

Activities are illustrative. Final experiences will reflect the camp schedule, local learning opportunities, and camper needs.

A day at Urban Earth

Room to wonder.
Time to make.

A full camp day makes space for discovery, collaboration, breaks, and reflection. Campers can explore a question in the morning and turn it into something they can build, explain, or share.

↳

An illustrative day
The final schedule will vary with activities and field experiences.

  1. 9:00 A.M.

    Arrive & connect

    Welcome, team check-in, and a question to spark the day.

  2. MORNING

    Explore & investigate

    Guided observation, an outdoor investigation, or a hands-on STEM workshop.

  3. MIDDAY

    Pause & recharge

    Lunch break, conversation, and time to reset with the group.

  4. AFTERNOON

    Create & collaborate

    Build, test, work on a team project, and reflect on discoveries.

  5. 3:00 P.M.

    Share & head home

    Close the day with a new connection and something to talk about.

Ask the camp team ↗
Who is Urban Earth Camp for?

The planning model serves middle school students entering grades 6–8 in fall 2027, with one cohort of approximately 30 campers.

When and where will camp take place?

We’re planning for Summer 2027, with 9 a.m.–3 p.m. day-camp hours over six weeks. The camp is being developed in Baltimore, connected to Phase 3’s training space and local outdoor learning opportunities. Final dates, site details, and drop-off information will be announced.

How do families register, and what will camp cost?

Registration information and fees are being finalized. Families can email info@phase3training.org to express interest and ask questions while details are developed.

Will there be field trips or a culminating experience?

Outdoor field experiences and a culminating camp experience are being explored. Destinations, transportation, permissions, and any overnight arrangements will be shared once confirmed.

What about meals, transportation, and support needs?

Meal and transportation arrangements are still being finalized. Please share accessibility or participation questions with the camp team when you inquire so we can discuss your family’s needs.

How does camp connect to the 12-week program?

Summer Camp is a separate summer offering built around Urban Earth’s six environmental learning themes. Its full-day format provides time for connected investigations and projects. You can also explore the school-year 12-week Journey.
